About CareerAI Nexus

AI-powered career intelligence built with transparency, privacy, and responsible AI principles at its core.

Our Purpose

CareerAI Nexus exists to democratize access to career intelligence. We believe everyone deserves clear, actionable insights about their professional growth — not just those with access to expensive career coaches or enterprise tools.

🧠 How AI Analysis Works

Our platform combines three advanced AI techniques:

  • Natural Language Processing (NLP): Extracts skills, experience, and qualifications from both your CV and job descriptions.
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Grounds recommendations in verified course data from trusted providers, reducing hallucinations.
  • AI Agent Workflow: Orchestrates multiple specialized tools — CV parsing, skill extraction, course research, and roadmap generation — into a coherent analysis pipeline.

📚 Course Recommendation Engine

We match skill gaps against a curated database of courses from providers like Coursera, edX, Udacity, and MIT OpenCourseWare. Each recommendation includes verified pricing, duration, and official source links.

✅ Source Verification

Every course in our database is periodically verified. If a price or availability cannot be confirmed, we display "Price could not be verified" rather than inventing data. Transparency over convenience.

🔒 Privacy & Responsible AI

Your CV data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We never sell personal data. AI-generated analyses include confidence scores so you know when to trust the output. You can delete your data at any time.
